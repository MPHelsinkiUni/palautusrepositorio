A good daily scrum is a scrum that has everyone knowing what to do and what direction to head to and with every member understanding their role, trusting each other and trusting themselves to find their niche in the project. The best kind would require a level of psychological safety (I like the word trust better) to describe your problems without fear of judgment and a level of empathy in approaching each topic or each person involved. This is reciprocal and it has to be for the project to function as a cohesive whole. It is concise, understanding, safe, comprehensive and directed.
